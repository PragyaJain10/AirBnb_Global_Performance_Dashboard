# Global Airbnb Performance Dashboard 📊

An interactive, multi-page Power BI dashboard designed to analyze the growth lifecycle, market share distribution, and guest satisfaction metrics across major global tourism hubs. This project turns complex hospitality data into actionable business strategies for rental platforms, property managers, and market analysts.

---

### 🚀 Project Overview
This project evaluates the business performance of Airbnb across **10 major global cities** encompassing **144 distinct property types**. By combining macroeconomic lifecycle tracking with granular customer experience metrics, the dashboard provides a 360-degree view of market maturity, pricing structures, and regional competitiveness.

### 🛠️ Tools & Technologies Used
* **Power BI Desktop:** Report building, conditional formatting, and dashboard architecture.
* **Power Query (ETL):** Data cleansing, filtering, and structuring.
* **Key Features:** Dynamic narrative insights, customized lifecycle trendlines, and interactive "Select Detail Level" view toggles.

### 📈 Core Metrics & KPIs Analyzed
* **Global Footprint:** `2,79,712` Total Listings across `10 Cities`.
* **Host Network:** `1,82,024` Total Hosts managing properties.
* **Engagement Volume:** Over `5,373K` Total Reviews tracked.
* **Room Formats:** Segmented by Entire Place, Private Room, Shared Room, and Hotel Room.

### 🔍 Key Dashboard Features & Architecture
1. **Market Lifecycle Analysis:** Tracks the velocity of new listings over structured growth stages (*Introduction, Growth, Maturity, Decline, Reinvention, and COVID-19 Impact*).
2. **City Market Share & Pricing Tiering:** Contrasts listing volume alongside host designations (*Superhost vs. Regular Host*), while evaluating average nightly rates across room types (ranging from `$800/night` for premium hotel rooms to `$462/night` for budget private rooms).
3. **Interactive Quality Scorecard:** Implements an advanced UI switch letting users toggle between high-level **Overall Ratings** charts and deep-dive **Detailed Matrices** across five core metrics: *Accuracy, Cleanliness, Communication, Location, and Value*.

### 💡 Core Business Insights Discovered
* **Dominant Hubs:** Paris, NYC, and Sydney form the structural core of the platform, driving nearly **50% of all global listings and 48% of total reviews**.
* **Market Dynamics:** Paris commands the highest listing volume, driven by local hotel rooms pricing out at double the cost of an average Airbnb.
* **Customer Sentiment:** Mexico City and Rio de Janeiro represent the overall **highest-rated cities**, while Hong Kong and Istanbul score the lowest. Universally, *Cleanliness* and *Value for Money* are the most volatile metrics requiring operational focus.
